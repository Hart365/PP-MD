# Code of Conduct

Treat participants with respect, patience, and consideration. Different backgrounds, access needs, and levels of experience belong here.

Give constructive, specific feedback. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish private information, or use sexualised language or imagery. Respect requests to stop and keep discussion focused on the project.

Report concerns privately through the contact route at [Hart of the Midlands](https://HartOfTheMidlands.co.uk), including only information needed to understand the concern. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or block accounts to protect the community. Reports will be handled with discretion; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
